Input image 1 is the site's new home-page STYLE REFERENCE. Carry its mint green, warm cream, evergreen text, tiny gold accents, clover logo, elegant spacing, subtle borders, and restrained rounded surfaces into a working administration interface. This page is for operating tables and forms. Do not add the home hero, a big bear, meadow artwork, oversized calligraphy, decorative grass, or new statistics. Use crisp sans-serif text for all workspace headings and table content; reserve the small handwritten brand wordmark for the header.
